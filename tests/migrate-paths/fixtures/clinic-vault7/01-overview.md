# 01 — Overview

Patients self-book, reschedule, and cancel appointments online; clinic staff
(doctors, receptionists) view and manage the daily schedule. One clinic,
5 doctors, ±50 appointments/day.

## Goals

- Patients self-book without calling the clinic.
- Reduce no-shows via a 24h email reminder.
- Online booking share ≥ 50% within 3 months (via `booking_channel`).
