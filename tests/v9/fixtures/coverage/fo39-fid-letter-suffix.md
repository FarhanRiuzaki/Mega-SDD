# PRD: Transfers
## F-TRF-001 Instant Transfer
Transfer to any BI-FAST bank settles in under 10 s.
### F-TRF-001a Scheduled Transfer
The customer picks a date; the transfer runs at 00:05 WIB.
### F-TRF-001b Recurring Transfer
Monthly on a chosen day; a failed month is retried the next business day.
## F-TRF-002 Transfer History
The last 90 days, newest first.
