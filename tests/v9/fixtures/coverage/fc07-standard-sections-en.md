# PRD: Checkout Revamp

**Author:** Jane PM · **Status:** Draft

## Overview
We are redesigning the checkout flow to reduce drop-off on mobile.

## Problem Statement
Mobile checkout conversion is 38% vs 61% on desktop.

## Goals
- Lift mobile conversion to 50%.

## Non-Goals
- Changing the payment provider.

## Success Metrics
| Metric | Baseline | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile conversion | 38% | 50% |

## Personas
- **Busy Parent** — buys groceries on the phone.

## Stakeholders
| Name | Role |
|---|---|
| Jane | PM |

## User Stories
### US-1 Guest checkout
As a guest I can check out without an account.
### US-2 Saved addresses
As a returning user I can pick a saved address.

## Assumptions
- Most users have a single shipping address.
