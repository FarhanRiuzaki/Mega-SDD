# PRD: Refund Desk

## 1. Refund Request
Customers request a refund from the order page.

## 2. Out of Scope
Automatic refunds stay out of v1, but ops MUST settle every manual refund within 2 business days.

### 2.1 Pending Refund Report
Ops sees a daily list of refunds older than 24 hours, sorted by amount.

### 2.2 Refund Reason Codes
Every refund carries one of five reason codes chosen from a dropdown.

## 3. Notifications
Customers get an e-mail when a refund is settled.
