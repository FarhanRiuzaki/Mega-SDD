# PRD: Pinjaman Online

## Pengajuan
Nasabah mengisi nominal dan tenor.

## Dokumen Pendukung
Nasabah mengunggah KTP, NPWP dan slip gaji 3 bulan terakhir.

## Meeting Notes
After each advisor call the app saves the notes to the customer profile.

## Schedule
Advisors set weekly availability; national holidays such as 17 August are blocked automatically.

## Q&A
Customers type a question, for example "Can I repay early?", and an advisor answers within one business day.

## Additional Information
Applicants may add free text up to 500 characters.

## Success
After disbursement the customer sees a success screen with the contract number.

## Hypotheses
Analysts record a hypothesis per campaign with its target metric.

## User Segments
Marketers build segments from age, income and region filters.

## Not Included
The basic plan excludes early-repayment waivers.

## OOS
Out-of-stock vouchers are hidden from the catalogue.

## Designs
Figma: https://figma.com/file/loan
### Login Screen
The login screen has phone number and PIN fields and a Forgot PIN link.

## Roadmap
### MVP (Q3)
Early repayment with a 1% fee.

## Early Repayment
Customers repay the whole principal before the tenor ends.
