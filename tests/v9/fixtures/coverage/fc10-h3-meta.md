# PRD: Wallet Top-up

## Introduction
### Background
Users top up via bank transfer today; it takes up to 1 day.
### Goals
Instant top-up via VA.
### Scope
Top-up only; withdrawals later.
### Out of Scope
Withdrawals.

## Requirements
### Create VA
The app creates a virtual account per user.
### Confirm payment
The app confirms payment on webhook.
