# Clinic Booking — 1-pager

## Description: What is it?

Online booking for clinic patients.

## Problem: What problem is this solving?

Booking calls go unanswered at peak hours.

## Why: How do we know this is a real problem and worth solving?

Call logs show 30% unanswered in May.

## Success: How do we know if we've solved this problem?

Half of bookings online within a quarter.

## Audience: Who are we building for?

Patients booking for themselves or family.

## What: Roughly, what does this look like in the product?

A patient picks a doctor and a free slot and confirms with name and phone; a patient cancels from the confirmation link.

## How: What is the experiment plan?

Launch to one clinic first.

## When: When does it ship and what are the milestones?

Beta July, GA August.
