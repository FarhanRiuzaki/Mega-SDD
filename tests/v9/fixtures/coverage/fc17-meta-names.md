# PRD: Clinic Booking

## Section 1: Overview
Online booking for the clinic's patients.

## Document Purpose
This PRD aligns product, design and engineering on the booking MVP.

## The Problem
30% of booking calls go unanswered at peak hours.

## Hypothesis
If patients can book online, unanswered calls drop by half.

## Opportunity
Competing clinics already take 20% of our walk-ins through online booking.

## Target Personas
- **Patient** — books from a phone.
