# PRD: Finance hub
## Accounts Receivable
### Open Items
Shows every unpaid invoice per customer, sortable by due date.
### Aging Report
Buckets receivables by 30/60/90 days.
## Issue Tracker
### Open Issues
Lists issues whose status is open, filterable by assignee.
### Isu Terbuka
Daftar isu berstatus terbuka per cabang.
